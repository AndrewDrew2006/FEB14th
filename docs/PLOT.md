# The Case of the Secret Admirer
## Complete Plot Documentation — Scene by Scene

**Tone:** Sweet & earnest (friendship, care) + Playful & silly (exaggerated detective language, funny case files)

**Atmosphere:** Ambient, cozy, pixelated high-quality game. Soft snow, warm lights, nostalgic Clarkson winter.

---

## OPENING SEQUENCE

### Scene 0: Title Card (2–3 seconds)
- **Visual:** Fade in from black. Pixel-art title appears letter by letter with a subtle typewriter effect.
- **Text:** `THE CASE OF THE SECRET ADMIRER`
- **Subtitle (smaller, delayed):** `A Clarkson University Mystery`
- **Ambience:** Very soft piano chord, held. Distant wind sfx.
- **Animation:** Light snow particles drift across the title. Screen gently pulses (breathing effect).
- **Tap prompt (appears after 1.5s):** `[ TAP TO BEGIN ]` — subtle bounce animation.

### Scene 2: The Quad — First Clue
**Duration:** ~15–20 seconds

**Visual:**
- Wide establishing shot of the Clarkson quad in winter.
- Snow-covered ground. Bare trees with delicate pixel branches.
- Warm glow from distant building windows (amber, soft).
- Footprints appear in the snow as if someone just walked through (light animation).
- A small envelope or folded note sits on a bench, highlighted with a soft gleam.

**Ambience:**
- Wind, gentle. Occasional snowflake. Very quiet.
- Optional: distant campus bells (1–2 chimes).

**Text (tap to advance):**

> **[LOCATION LOG — ENTRY 1]**
>
> *The Quad. February 14th. Overcast.*

*[Tap]*

> You arrive at the heart of campus. The snow is fresh. The air is cold. Your breath fogs in the pixel-art air.
>
> Something catches your eye: a note, left on a bench.

*[Tap]*

> You pick it up. It reads:
>
> *"Where pages turn and silence wins,*
> *where students chase their might-have-beens —*
> *go there next. The clue begins."*

*[Tap]*

> **CASE NOTE:** Obviously the library. You're a brilliant detective. Obviously.

**Interaction:** Tap the note to "pick it up" — it flutters into the inventory/case file. Screen transitions with a page-turn or location-blur effect.

### Scene 6: The River — The Final Clue
**Duration:** ~25–30 seconds (emotional peak)

**Visual:**
- Exterior: Raquette River (or generic river/bridge near campus). Snow on the banks.
- Bare trees. Gray sky. A single bench by the water.
- A small box or envelope rests on the bench. Soft spotlight or glow on it.
- Snow falls slowly. Very still, very peaceful.

**Ambience:**
- Wind. Water (gentle flow). Distant campus in the background.
- Music swells softly — warm, hopeful. Not sad.

**Text (tap to advance):**

> **[LOCATION LOG — FINAL ENTRY]**
>
> *The River. The bridge. Evening.*

*[Tap]*

>
> On the bench: one last envelope. Your name on it.

*[Tap]*

> You open it.

*[Tap]*

> *"The gift was never lost.*
> *It was always yours.*
>
> *Someone who cares about you — a lot — left it right here. Not because you had to solve puzzles. But because you're worth a little adventure. And a little mystery. And a little extra thought."*

*[Tap]*

> *"Happy Valentine's, detective.*
> *You solved it. You always do."*

*[Tap]*

> **CASE NOTE:** Case closed. With a smile.

**Transition:** Envelope opens. Golden light spills out. Fade to reveal scene.
